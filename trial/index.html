The lanes and hazards are the Safety Committee's Google map, copied recently, drawn exactly where the committee placed them; tap any of them for its note. The photos are the State of Connecticut's, taken in spring 2023 before the leaves came out, so the banks show: 3-inch detail, accurate to about 4 inches. The wind is one forecast for the whole lake, at the boathouse dock, now and for the next 48 hours: the banks shelter some stretches, and gusts vary. The streaks drift with it over the water; the longer, faster ones are the gusts. Headwind, crosswind and tailwind are worked out from the direction of each stretch of the lanes.
